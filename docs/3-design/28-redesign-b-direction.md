# Redesign B: direction contract

A design exploration only. Preview: `archive/prototype/design-options/redesign-b.html`. Nothing here is on the live site.
Made with the Impeccable skill's process (new visual world, Persuade mode for the diner pages, Operate mode for the dashboard). The skill's own helper program was not run; its steps were done by hand.

## How the direction was chosen

- **What only khabo does:** real, checked menu prices, so a diner knows the bill before going.
- **The rut to avoid:** the food-app page (big food photo, search bar, rows of equal cards), its dark fine-dining opposite, and Redesign A (neutral grey, one indigo accent, pill buttons).
- **Seven things diners in Bangladesh know by heart:** Old Dhaka hand-painted price signboards; rickshaw-art enamel colours; the restaurant cash memo pad; taka notes; jamdani weave; Boishakh alpona; Bangladesh Railway tickets.
- **Picked:** the painted price board (it is literally a shop showing its prices) joined with the cash memo (the bill you see before you sit).

## Contract

- **THESIS:** khabo is the town's price board. The first thing you see is the board itself, working, not a hero photo with a search box.
- **OWN-WORLD:** signboard bottle green owns whole regions, enamel red for actions, marigold for prices only, cool paper for reading areas. One type family with a width axis (Anek Bangla): condensed heavy letters like painted boards, normal width for reading. Small 8px corners, a thin inset frame line like a board's border.
- **STORY:** see a dish's price across town, open a place, build the bill on the cash memo, go.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** a full-width green board. Headline painted across the top. A sentence control: "Show me [dish] in [area]". Under it, every place with its price in big marigold numbers, cheapest first, a line saying the typical price.
- **FORM:** signboard plus cash memo. Restaurant pages swap the board to the restaurant's own colours. The dashboard keeps standard controls; the board appears once, as "your price on the area board".
- **SIGNATURE MOTION:** when the dish or area changes, the prices repaint left to right, one row after another.
- **FINISH:** reviewed by a fresh reviewer against this contract, with screenshots at laptop and phone, light and dark.
