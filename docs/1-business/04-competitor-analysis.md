# Competitor Analysis

**Date:** November 2026 · Review every quarter.
**Note:** Facts come from the competitors' own websites and press coverage. Their claims (customer numbers etc.) are not independently verified.

---

## 1. Summary
- **No local competitor** combines dine-in discovery, real current prices, verified reviews and seeing inside the restaurant.
- The QR-menu companies sell **operations software** to individual restaurants. They do not bring diners.
- The closest local idea is **Dinebd** (restaurant listings with menus and reviews). Test it.
- The biggest competitor for diner attention is **free**: Facebook food groups and Google Maps.
- Bangladesh's delivery market has seen several exits (Uber Eats 2020, Shohoz Food 2021, HungryNaki 2023). Lesson: growth funded only by discounts doesn't survive. We charge restaurants from the start and keep costs low.

## 2. Local competitors (Bangladesh)

### 2.1 Dine-in discovery and reviews (closest to us)
| Name | What they do | Threat | Our difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dinebd** (app) | Restaurant listings with menu, photos, ratings, reviews, awards; ordering | ⚠️ High (same idea area) | Check how current their prices are, whether reviews are verified, whether they have 360°/video/3D and AI. Download and test it. |
| **FoodBank** (Facebook group) | Large Dhaka food review community; restaurants offer member discounts tied to reviews | ⚠️ High for diner attention | Reviews are unstructured and hard to search; no menus or prices. We can **link public Facebook posts** on restaurant pages. |
| **Food Monsters** (Chattogram FB group) | Large review group (2.26 lakh+ members reported) | Medium (Chattogram) | Same as above; future city expansion |
| **Priyo Review** | Yelp-style review site launched ~2015 | Low (status unknown) | |
| **Google Maps** | Listings, user photos, reviews | ⚠️ High for search | Old photos, unverified reviews, menus rarely complete |

### 2.2 QR menu & restaurant operations software
| Name | What they do | Price | Overlap | Our difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **iCafe** (Dhaka, Bashundhara) | QR ordering + POS for food courts and cafés; kitchen screen; analytics; AR "3D menu" in top tier | ৳800 / ৳1,650 / ৳2,499 / ৳2,999 / ৳4,500 per month; 14-day free trial; 15% off yearly | QR menu, analytics, 3D menu | No diner discovery, price comparison, verified reviews or 360° |
| **QRDine** (Dhaka) | QR ordering with bKash, Nagad, Rocket; claims 500+ restaurants (unverified) | Flat monthly (no public figure) | QR menu | Operations only |
| **HisabPlus** | Restaurant POS with QR table ordering; covers Dhaka, Chattogram, Bogura and others | — | QR menu, present in Bogura | Operations only |
| **Menumium** (6amTech, Dhaka) | Sells restaurant SaaS **source code** to entrepreneurs | Not public | Indirect: someone could copy us quickly with it | Our moat is network + data, not code |
| **Freelancers & agencies** | Restaurant websites | ৳50,000–150,000 one-time | Online presence | ৳1,999/month on a platform people visit |

## 3. International competitors and examples to learn from
These are not in Bangladesh today but show what works.

| Name | Country | What they do | Lesson for us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zomato** | India | Started as a menu-and-restaurant discovery site (scanned menus), then reviews, then delivery and dining-out | Menus + discovery can build a huge brand; starting with menus is valid |
| **Swiggy Dineout / EazyDiner** | India | Dine-in discovery, table booking, dining deals | Dine-in deals and bookings are a real revenue line (our future phase) |
| **Dianping (Meituan)** | China | Reviews, menus, photos and deals for dine-in | Trusted reviews + deals at huge scale; closest model to our long-term vision |
| **Tabelog** | Japan | Trusted restaurant ratings with anti-manipulation focus | Trust in reviews *is* the product |
| **Yelp** | USA | Local business reviews for every category | Same model expands from restaurants to all local businesses (our long-term plan) |
| **TripAdvisor** | Global | Reviews, rankings, photos | Strong for tourists; weak on menus/prices |
| **TheFork / OpenTable** | Europe / USA | Table booking + discovery | Bookings revenue (future) |
| **Burpple** | Singapore | Community food reviews + deals membership | Deals membership as revenue |
| **Eatigo** | SE Asia | Time-based dine-in discounts | Fill empty off-peak tables (future idea) |
| **Restaurant Guru, Foodle** | Global | Aggregated listings and reviews, incl. Dhaka pages | Aggregators exist but data is thin locally |
| **MenuTiger** | Global (USD) | QR + online ordering + POS; Free / $17 / $46 / $119 per month by order volume | Higher price because it's a full ordering/POS system for global markets |

### 3D / AR menu companies (for our 3D dish menu)
| Name | What they do | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| **QReal (formerly Kabaq)** | Photorealistic 3D food models by scanning; WebAR (no app needed); worked with Bareburger, Magnolia Bakery, Denny's | 3D food works in the browser without an app. It's still rare, so it's a differentiator for us |
| **HoloMenu, Dine AR** | AR menu platforms | Technology exists; cost and capture method are the challenge |

## 5. Threats and our response
| Threat | Response |
|---|---|
| Dinebd improves | Move faster on content quality, verified reviews, AI, prices |
| QR companies add discovery | Our network density and diner brand; they're built for operations |
| foodpanda adds dine-in | Focus on verified prices, kitchen/360° content and local relationships |
| Copycat built from source code (e.g. Menumium) | Moats: data history, reviews, contracts, Price Index authority |
| Facebook groups stay dominant | Work *with* them: link public posts, partner with group admins and food bloggers |
