# Value Proposition Canvas

Visual version: open **[canvases.html](canvases.html)** in a browser.

The canvas has two sides. **Right:** what the customer needs (jobs, pains, gains). **Left:** how our product meets it (products, pain relievers, gain creators). The goal is a clear fit between the two.

---

## 1. Diners

### Customer profile
| Jobs to be done | Pains | Gains |
|---|---|---|
| Choose where to eat | Don't know the final bill (VAT and service charge surprises) | Confidence before going |
| Fit the meal to a budget and group size | Old, edited or fake photos | Saving money |
| Avoid a bad experience | Paid or fake reviews | A good time with family and friends |
| Find a place for an occasion (birthday, iftar, office) | Hard to find family or group-friendly places (cabin, prayer room, parking) | Discovering something new |
| | Delivery app prices ≠ dine-in prices | |

### Value map
| Products & services | Pain relievers | Gain creators |
|---|---|---|
| Search with many filters | Prices with "updated X days ago" | Plan a meal to a budget in seconds (AI) |
| Full menus with real prices | Bill calculator with VAT and service charge | Compare dish prices across restaurants |
| Photo, video or 3D dish menus | Real, dated photos and video | See inside before going (360°) |
| Promo video, kitchen media, 360° tour | Verified reviews | Deals and the Food Price Index |
| AI assistant in Bangla and English | Facility filters | |
| Verified reviews + linked Facebook posts | | |

**Fit statement:** *Diners know exactly what they'll eat, what it costs and what the place is like before they leave home.*

## 2. Restaurants

### Customer profile
| Jobs to be done | Pains | Gains |
|---|---|---|
| Fill tables | Websites cost ৳50,000+ and nobody visits them | More customers |
| Look professional online | Reprinting menus when prices change | Trust and reputation |
| Keep menus and prices up to date | No time or skill for photos and video | Insight into customers and competitors |
| Stay competitive in the area | Fake or unfair reviews | Low, predictable monthly cost |
| | No data about what customers want | |

### Value map
| Products & services | Pain relievers | Gain creators |
|---|---|---|
| Listing + full menu page | No expensive website needed | Seen by people deciding where to eat |
| QR menu + Print menu | Paper menu always matches online, no reprint waste | Know which dishes people look at |
| Photo, video, 360°, 3D content | We shoot everything | Know how their prices compare |
| Monthly report | Verified reviews reduce unfair attacks | Professional brand image |
| Competitor price insights | | Featured placement and Deal of the Week (higher tiers) |
| Deals, featured placement, social promotion | | |

**Fit statement:** *A complete, professional online presence seen by people choosing where to eat, for less than one plate a day.*

## 3. Our own pains (business risks)
| Risk | How we plan to reduce it |
|---|---|
| Prices go out of date | Monthly check calls; self-serve dashboard in phase 2; staleness warnings |
| Content shoots are costly | Standard shoot playbook; commitment period or setup fee (open question) |
| Collecting monthly payments | bKash / Nagad links, prepay discounts, reminders |
| Low early traffic | Launch area by area; Food Price Index PR; SEO; social content |
| Copycats | Network density, data history, verified reviews, contracts (see Vision §8) |
