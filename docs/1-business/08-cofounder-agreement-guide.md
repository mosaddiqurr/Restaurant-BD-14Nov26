# Co-founder Agreement Guide

**This is a guide, not a legal contract.** Use it to agree on the important questions together, then have a Bangladeshi corporate lawyer turn the answers into a signed agreement (and later a Shareholders' Agreement when the company is registered with RJSC).

**Why do this now:** most startup fights are about equity, roles and someone leaving. Agreeing early, while everyone is friendly, prevents that. Investors will also ask for it.

## 2. Equity (ownership)
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| How will equity be split? | |
| What is it based on? (idea, time, money invested, skills, risk) | |
| Will we keep an option pool for future key employees? (commonly 10–15%) | |

**Common approaches:**
- **Equal split:** simple, works when everyone contributes similarly and full-time.
- **Weighted split:** based on role, commitment, money and expertise.
- Avoid deciding by "who had the idea". Execution matters more.

---

## 3. Vesting (earning equity over time)
**Standard practice:** equity vests over **4 years with a 1-year cliff.**
- Leave before 1 year: no equity.
- After 1 year: 25% vests, then the rest monthly over the next 3 years.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do we use 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff? | |
| Does vesting speed up if the company is sold (acceleration)? | |

## 7. Intellectual property (IP)
- Everything created for [Brand] (code, designs, brand, content, data, photos, videos, 3D models) **belongs to the company**, not to individuals.
- Every founder signs an **IP assignment**.

## 10. Steps to make it official
1. Discuss and fill in this guide together.
2. Write a simple **Founders' Term Sheet** (1–2 pages of the answers).
3. Hire a **corporate lawyer** to draft the Co-founder Agreement and IP Assignments.
4. When registering a **limited company (RJSC)**, reflect the equity in the share allotment and the **Articles of Association**.
5. Before investment, prepare a **Shareholders' Agreement** and **cap table**.
6. Review the agreement every year or when roles change.
