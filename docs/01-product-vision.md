# Product Vision Document

**Product:** [Brand] (name not decided)
**Version:** Draft 1
**Markets at launch:** Dhaka and Bogura, Bangladesh

---

## 1. Vision
> **To become Bangladesh's trusted place to see, compare and choose where to go, starting with restaurants.**

Long term, every district of Bangladesh (all 64), then other local businesses: cafés, bakeries, salons and shops.

## 2. Mission
Give every diner the truth before they leave home: **the real menu, the real price, the real place and real reviews.** Give every restaurant an affordable, professional online presence that brings customers.

## 3. One-line positioning
> **See the real menu, real prices and the real place before you go.**

Category: **Bangladesh's dine-in discovery network.**
Others sell restaurants software. **We bring restaurants customers and trust.**

---

## 4. The problem

### Diners
- Don't know what a meal will cost until the bill arrives (VAT and service charge are surprises).
- Online photos are old, edited or fake.
- Reviews in Facebook groups and on Google are mixed with paid or fake ones.
- Hard to find a place that fits a family, a group, a budget or an occasion.
- Delivery apps show delivery prices, not dine-in prices.

### Restaurants
- A website costs ৳50,000+ and nobody visits it.
- Menus must be reprinted every time prices change.
- No professional photos or video; no time to make them.
- They can't see what customers want or how their prices compare.
- Unfair or fake reviews damage them.

## 6. What we offer

### For diners (always free)
- Search with many filters: area, cuisine, budget, price per person, occasion, facilities, dietary, group size, open now, rating, deals and more
- Dish search and price comparison across restaurants
- Full menu with real prices and "updated X days ago"
- Menu formats chosen by the restaurant: **real photo menu, video menu or 3D dish menu**
- 20–30 second promo video for each restaurant
- Professional kitchen photos and video, with the capture date (no hygiene badge)
- 360° / virtual tour
- Bill calculator with VAT and service charge
- AI assistant in Bangla, English and Banglish, including budget planning ("৳2,500, 4 people")
- Verified reviews, plus public Facebook posts about the restaurant linked to the original
- Deals and offers
- Monthly Food Price Index (Dhaka and Bogura)
- Bangla and English everywhere

### For restaurants (paid: Starter, Pro, Premium)
- Complete online presence on a platform people use
- Table QR menu, and a **Print menu** button so paper and online always match
- Professional photos, video, 360° and 3D content made by our team
- Monthly report: views, QR scans, top dishes
- Competitor price insights
- Deals, featured placement, Deal of the Week, social promotion (higher tiers)

## 8. Making us hard to copy (moats)
Features can be copied. These are much harder to copy:
1. **Network density:** many restaurants per area plus diners who use us. A copycat starts at zero.
2. **Data history:** months of verified price history, the Food Price Index and dish-level data.
3. **Verified review base:** real reviews collected over time cannot be faked quickly.
4. **Content library:** professional photos, videos, 360° and 3D for every restaurant, with media rights agreed in our contracts.
5. **Relationships and contracts:** personal account management, 6–12 month agreements, founding partner status.
6. **Brand and authority:** the Food Price Index makes us the reference for food prices in the press.
7. **Operational speed:** a repeatable onboarding and shoot playbook lets us add restaurants faster than anyone.

---

## 9. Principles (what we never do)
- **Never fake anything:** no AI-faked interiors or dishes, no fake reviews, no inflated numbers.
- **Never promise inspections we don't do:** we show the kitchen; diners judge.
- **Never force digital:** paper menus are supported and always match.
- **Mark paid placement clearly** as "Featured".
- **Prices must be current:** stale data breaks our promise.

- 8–10 restaurants per launched area before adding the next
- Over 90% of menus updated in the last 30 days
- Diner traffic growing month on month; first Food Price Index published and covered by press
