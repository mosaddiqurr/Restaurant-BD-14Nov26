What every concept must get right

Before style: people choose a place on a few facts. If those facts are on every card, in the same order, any concept becomes easy to use.

The six facts that decide a restaurant

  1. Price per person as a range (৳450-650), never "৳৳".
  2. Travel time in traffic now (18 min), not kilometres. In Dhaka, distance is minutes.
  3. Open until (11:30 pm), so nobody drives to a closed door.
  4. Rating with Verified, so the number can be trusted.
  5. The one facility that matters for this search (family cabin, prayer room, parking, rooftop).
  6. How fresh the prices are (checked 2 days ago). This is our whole promise.

Ten concepts, compared on use

Taps are my estimate of the fewest taps from opening the site to having the answer (typing a query counts as one). Scores are 1-10 judgements from a product-design view; they are a starting point to test, not facts. Sorted by UX score.

No single concept wins

The fastest (One Box) is weak at discovery. The most delightful (Reels) is the slowest and heaviest. The most guided (Occasion) is slow for people in a hurry. Great products combine a fast entry with several ways to look.

What Bangladesh changes

Traffic makes minutes matter more than distance. Groups decide in WhatsApp. Mobile data is precious. Ramadan reshapes eating for a month. Concepts that respect these four facts score higher.

Owners matter too

Restaurants pay us, so the product must show them off: video, 360°, kitchen, guides. Reels and Guides do this best; the blueprint keeps them inside restaurant pages and on the home instead of as the whole app.

What it takes from each concept

One Box (1)Search and AI merged into one box. Nobody has to choose between "search" and "ask AI".
Right Now (9)A home that changes by time and season; iftar becomes a signature moment.
Map Sheet (2) and Dish Wall (3)Become lenses on the same results instead of separate apps.
Budget Dial (4)Budget per person is a first-class chip and the AI understands "৳2,500 for 4".
Occasion (5) and Group Plan (10)Merge into Plans: start from an occasion, share a link, vote, split the bill.
Compare Pro (8)A compare tray for up to three places, for planners and office groups.
Guides (7)A home row and SEO pages, written by our team.
Reels (6)Video lives inside restaurant pages and Guides, with data saver on by default.

One change to the current plan, for you to approve: the bottom bar becomes Home · Search · Plans · Deals · Account. "Ask AI" moves into the search box (same power, one less decision), which frees a slot for Plans, the feature with built-in growth.

Prove it before building

How a top product team would decide: clickable prototypes of the three strongest options, tested with real people, measured the same way.

Who (15 people)6 young diners in Dhaka, 4 Dhaka families including two people over 50, 3 diners in Bogura, 2 restaurant owners.
WhatPrototypes of the Blueprint, Occasion Plans and Right Now, each in Bangla and English, on a mid-range Android phone.
TasksThe three tasks above, plus "plan iftar for 6" and "send two options to your family group".
MeasureTask success, time, taps, a 1-7 ease rating per task, and which version they would keep. Decide on data, not taste.

What you decide next

  1. Structure: the Blueprint, one of the ten concepts, or your own mix (for example "Blueprint, but with the Budget Dial on the home").
  2. Bottom bar: keep "Ask AI" as its own tab, or merge it into search and add Plans.
  3. What ships first: I suggest One Box + List/Map lenses + restaurant page + QR menu at launch; Plans, Guides and the Dishes lens within the next weeks.
  4. Look: pick from file 1 (18-design-directions.html). Any look there can sit on any structure here.
  5. Testing: whether to run the 15-person prototype test before the build (recommended; about a week).
