# Strategic Product Roadmap

Phases are based on milestones, not fixed dates. Move to the next phase when its exit criteria are met.

---

## Phase 0: Validate & Prepare
**Goal:** prove restaurants will pay and prepare to build.
- Talk to 20+ restaurant owners in Dhaka and Bogura ([approach guide](../1-business/06-restaurant-approach-guide.md))
- Decide: brand name, domain, tech stack, design system, pricing open questions
- Finish documents: architecture, CLAUDE.md, wireframes, brand guide
- Co-founder agreement; trade licence
- Buy / rent basic shoot equipment; test the 360° and 3D capture methods

**Exit criteria:** 5–10 restaurants say they will pay (signed interest or trial agreement); key decisions made.

---

## Phase 1: Launch (MVP)
**Goal:** a live platform with 30–50 restaurants in Dhaka and Bogura.

**Diners:** search with filters, dish search and price comparison, restaurant pages (menu, prices, updated date, photo menu, promo video, kitchen media), bill calculator, AI assistant with budget planning, verified reviews, deals, Bangla/English.
**Diners (added 14 Nov 2026):** home page map with every partner restaurant pinned and labelled (PRD D-7).
**Restaurants:** restaurant panel where owners edit only their own restaurant: menu, prices, sold-out toggle, page details, photos, deals, review replies (moved from Phase 2, founder 15 Nov 2026), QR menu + print menu, QR table cards, branded QR codes with the restaurant's logo, colours and shape (PRD Q-8), monthly report with competitor price insights, featured placement, Deal of the Week.
**Team:** admin panel (menus, media, price history, reviews, deals, leads, billing, staleness alerts).
**Added 16 Nov 2026:** menu from a photo: owners and the team upload a photo of the paper menu, AI makes a draft, a person checks it before it goes live (PRD RD-6).
**Areas:** Dhaka (Dhanmondi, Gulshan/Banani, Mirpur, Uttara and more) + Bogura (Jaleshwaritola). Aim for 8–10 restaurants per area.

**Exit criteria:** 30–50 paying restaurants; menus > 90% current; steady diner growth.

## Phase 1.5: Fast follow (weeks after launch)
- 360° / virtual tours
- Video dish menus and first 3D dish menus
- Facebook posts on restaurant pages
- Compare restaurants, full map page with filters (the home page map is already in Phase 1)
- Start from the user's location: ask once, set the nearest area as the default, user can change it (PRD D-8)
- Owners choose their QR style and upload a logo themselves from the dashboard (PRD Q-9)
- Saved favourites, follow restaurants
- **Food Price Index** (first edition: e.g. "The Kacchi Report")
- Seasonal guides (Ramadan iftar, Eid)
- **Price tracker** page: dish prices over time, area averages, biggest rises and drops; shares one page with the Food Price Index (PRD D-9)
- **Buffet finder and compare** (PRD D-10)
- **Trending this week**, from real views and QR scans only (PRD D-11)
- **Events at restaurants**, added by owners in the panel (PRD D-12)

---

## Phase 2: Grow (around 50–100+ restaurants)
**Goal:** scale without the team doing every update by hand.
- **Restaurant panel, more:** detailed analytics, staff roles, billing inside the panel (the basic panel ships in Phase 1)
- **NFC table cards**
- Deal and price-drop alerts; group shortlist and voting; bill split with bKash/Nagad
- Automatic payment confirmation through a gateway
- **My lists:** make, rank and share lists of places (PRD U-7)
- **Digital stamp cards** ("Buy 5, get 1 free"), staff add stamps from the panel (PRD L-1)
- **Voice reviews in Bangla** (PRD V-6)
- **AI price advice** in the panel and monthly report (PRD RD-7)
- **Automatic Google Maps and Facebook sync** (PRD RD-8)
- **Own web address** for the restaurant's khabo website, e.g. shahihandi.com (PRD RD-9)
- More Dhaka areas; more Bogura coverage
- First investment round (if needed)

**Exit criteria:** restaurants update their own menus; low churn; growing repeat diners.

---

## Phase 3: Expand the product
- **Native mobile app** (Android first, then iOS)
- **QR ordering and call-waiter** from the table
- Table and group/event bookings; corporate dining
- Rewards for verified visits across restaurants
- **How busy right now** (PRD D-13)
- **Scan a menu** with the phone camera (PRD D-14)
- **Friends:** follow friends and see their lists (PRD U-8)
- **Pay your bill through khabo** with a restaurant-funded discount, via a payment gateway; free for diners (PRD PAY-1)
- Connections with POS systems (menu and price sync)
- WhatsApp bot
- Detailed visit / conversion tracking for restaurants
- New cities (e.g. Chattogram, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Khulna)

---

## Phase 4: Expand the market
- All **64 districts**
- **Other businesses:** cafés, bakeries, salons, shops: "see it before you go" for everything
- English tourist and diaspora guide
- Aggregated data reports for brands and investors
- Maybe: **restaurant job board** (PRD J-1, founder not sure yet)

---

## Ongoing in every phase
- Keep menus and prices current (our core promise)
- Monthly competitor check
- Collect restaurant and diner feedback; adjust the roadmap every quarter
