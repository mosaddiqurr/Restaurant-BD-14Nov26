# Restaurant Approach Guide

How to approach restaurant owners, what to ask, how to pitch and how to handle objections.
Goal of early visits: **learn first, sell second.** Every visit should teach us something, even when there's no sale.

---

## 1. Before the visit
- **Pick the right time:** 3–5 pm (after lunch, before dinner). Never at peak hours.
- **Ask for the owner or manager** who makes decisions.
- **Research for 5 minutes:** check their Facebook page, Google Maps photos and reviews, foodpanda listing. Note what's missing or outdated.
- **Bring:** a phone with the demo (or mock-up) ready, the one-pager, a printed sample QR card, and the visit record sheet (section 8).
- **Dress neatly**, and be respectful and brief.

---

## 2. Opening (first 30 seconds)
> "Assalamu Alaikum. I'm ___ from [Brand]. We're building a platform where people in [area] can see a restaurant's real menu, prices and photos before they visit. Can I take 10 minutes of your time? I'd like to learn how you get customers today."

Bangla version:
> "আসসালামু আলাইকুম। আমি [Brand] থেকে ___। আমরা এমন একটা প্ল্যাটফর্ম বানাচ্ছি যেখানে মানুষ রেস্টুরেন্টে যাওয়ার আগেই আসল মেনু, দাম আর ছবি দেখতে পারবে। ১০ মিনিট সময় দিবেন? আপনারা এখন কিভাবে কাস্টমার পান, সেটা জানতে চাই।"

---

## 3. Discovery questions (listen 70%, talk 30%)

### How they get customers
1. How do most new customers find you today? (Facebook, Google, walk-in, word of mouth, foodpanda?)
2. Do you spend money on marketing? Roughly how much a month, and on what (Facebook boosts, influencers, flyers)?
3. Which brings the best results?

### Online presence
4. Do you have a website? What did it cost? Do people visit it?
5. Who manages your Facebook page? How often do you post?
6. Are your Google Maps photos and information correct?

### Menu & prices
7. How often do your prices change?
8. How much does reprinting menus cost you each time?
9. Have customers ever complained that prices online were different?

### Reviews & reputation
10. Have you had unfair or fake reviews? What happened?
11. How do you handle complaints in Facebook groups?

### Data & competition
12. Do you know which dishes customers look at most, or why some dishes don't sell?
13. Do you know how your prices compare with nearby restaurants?

### Willingness to pay
14. If a service brought you new customers and handled your menu, photos and online presence, what would that be worth to you per month?
15. What would you need to see to try it?

**Write down their exact words.** They're the best material for our marketing and pitch.

---

## 4. The pitch (2 minutes)
Use what they told you. Example structure:
1. **Their problem in their words:** "You said reprinting menus costs ৳__ and your website gets no visitors…"
2. **What we do:** "On [Brand], people searching for food in [area] see your full menu, real prices, photos and video. Your table QR and printed menu always match."
3. **Show the demo** on your phone: a restaurant page, search filters, the QR menu.
4. **Why us:** "Other QR menus only serve people already inside. We bring the people who aren't here yet."
5. **Price:** "From ৳1,999 a month, about ৳67 a day, less than one plate of biryani."
6. **Offer:** the trial or founding partner offer.

---

## 5. Objections and answers

| They say | We answer |
|---|---|
| "I already have a QR menu." | "Your QR menu serves people already sitting here. Ours brings the people who aren't here yet, and your QR and paper menus stay matched." |
| "I have a Facebook page; that's enough." | "Keep it. We'll link to it. But people searching for 'kacchi under ৳400 in Dhanmondi' won't find a Facebook page. They'll find us." |
| "It's too expensive." | "It's ৳67 a day. One extra table a week covers it. And there's a free trial, so you can see it first." |
| "How many people use your site?" | Be honest: "We're launching in [area] now. That's why founding partners get the best price, locked for 12 months." |
| "I'm too busy." | "We do everything: photos, menu entry, QR cards. You only approve the menu." |
| "What if prices change?" | "Send us a WhatsApp and we update it the same day. Your printed menu updates too." |
| "foodpanda already shows my menu." | "foodpanda shows delivery prices to people ordering home. We show dine-in prices and your place to people deciding where to go out." |
| "I don't trust online reviews." | "Neither do diners. That's why our reviews are verified." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. Can I come back on [day]? Meanwhile, here's a sample of how your page could look." |

---

## 6. Closing
- Ask directly: "Shall we start your free trial this week?"
- If yes: agree on the **shoot date**, collect the menu (photo of it), contact details and the decision-maker's WhatsApp.
- If not ready: get permission to follow up, and ask "What would make this a yes for you?"
- **Validation goal for the first 20 visits:** 5–10 owners say they would pay (ideally a signed interest form or trial).

---

## 7. After the visit
- Send a thank-you WhatsApp the same day.
- Record the visit (section 8) within an hour.
- Follow up within 3 days if they said "maybe".
- Weekly team review: what objections came up most? What should change in the product or pitch?

## 9. Rules
- Never promise numbers we can't prove (views, customers).
- Never criticise competitors by name; explain our difference.
- Never pressure; respect their time.
- Always say clearly what's included and what costs extra.
